Fuda vintage cards

← Card studio · Real cards from 1909 to 1911, pictures that are in the public domain. Browse them in any display: flip them off the deck, fan them out.

The pictures are loaded from their public sources when this page opens; Fuda does not host or ship them. Each card was checked one at a time: Wikimedia Commons says "Public domain" on every hockey file used, and the Met marks every baseball object isPublicDomain. Neighbouring baseball cards the Met does not mark were left out. The cards show the clubs and makers of their day, as printed; the package carries none of them.
